Last updated: February 15, 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect your device identifier for account creation, the player name you choose, email address (optional, for account linking only), and gameplay data such as progress, statistics, and chat messages. We do not collect location data, contacts, or photos from your device.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to operate and improve the game, provide technical support, enforce community rules and ensure fair play, and send game-related notifications (if enabled).

3. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored on secure servers. Passwords are encrypted and cannot be viewed by anyone. We take reasonable security measures to protect your data, but no internet transmission can be guaranteed 100% secure.

4. Third-Party Services

We use the Apple App Store for payment processing. We may use analytics services to understand how the game is used. We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

5. Children's Privacy

The game is intended for ages 13 and above. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If we learn that we have collected data from a child, we will delete it immediately.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to request a copy of your personal data, request correction of inaccurate data, request deletion of your account and data, and opt out of notifications at any time.

7. Data Retention

We retain your data as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, your personal data is removed within 30 days. We may retain some anonymized data for analytical purposes.

8. International Users

Your data may be processed on servers outside your country. By using the game, you consent to the transfer of your data to these servers.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes through the game. Your continued use of the game constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@tajroba.sa or through the Support form in the game Settings.
